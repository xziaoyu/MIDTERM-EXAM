THE STORY BEHIND AMUMA

A BISAYA NAME. A HUMAN PURPOSE.

Amuma: care and nurture.

Amuma takes inspiration from the Bisaya term associated with care and nurture. My concept cares for both people's voices and their personal boundaries.

The goal is to let people connect, share ideas, and express themselves freely while minimizing unnecessary collection of personal information.

“Sa Amuma, importante ang imong tingog ug ang imong pribasiya.” At Amuma, your voice and your privacy matter.

Respect privacy

Collect less and explain clearly where demo information goes.

Make room for voices

Encourage self-expression while respecting other people's dignity.

Nurture connection

Build around shared stories and caring communities.

How this prototype works

Built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript only. There is no backend, database, or API. All images are bundled locally.

Fictional posts, likes, comments, and profile details are remembered in this browser when storage is available. Login and signup demonstrate form validation; passwords and emails are discarded.

This frontend cannot provide real authentication, encryption, access restrictions, or protection against copying and AI scraping. Use fictional information only.
